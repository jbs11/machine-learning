📖 Why Walls Shift Throughout the Day

📈 New Option Volume

Large new call or put positions at fresh strikes concentrate gamma. Heavy call buying at higher strikes relocates the Call Wall upward as dealer hedging pivots.

🔄 Contract Rolling

Institutions rolling hedges or closing positions reshapes open interest. Put walls often shift more intraday as puts are closed and dealers buy back short delta.

⏳ Time Decay (Theta)

As expiration approaches — especially 0DTE — gamma concentrates around ATM. Flip and walls tighten toward spot; small OI changes produce large level jumps.

🚀 Breakouts

When spot breaks a wall with force, formerly OTM strikes become ITM. The zone of dealer influence migrates; new walls form at the next OI cluster.

🟢 Call Wall (Ceiling)

Strike with highest call gamma — structural resistance. Dealers sell into rallies here. Intraday rallies can spawn new call walls at higher strikes.

🔴 Put Wall (Floor)

Strike with highest put gamma — structural support. Put walls tend to shift more frequently as hedges are adjusted through the session.

📚 Terminology

Call Wall

Strike with the largest positive call gamma exposure. Market makers hedging short calls sell the underlying into rallies — creating a structural ceiling where upside often stalls.

Put Wall

Strike with the largest put gamma concentration. Dealer put hedges create a floor where dips often find mechanical buying support.

Gamma Flip (HVL)

The zero-gamma / high-volatility level. Above flip = positive gamma (stabilizing); below = negative gamma (amplifying). Flip shifts are regime-change signals.

Overnight Shift

Change vs the last snapshot before today's 9:30 ET open. Captures globex flow, Asia/Europe sessions, and pre-market OI updates — usually the largest moves.

Intraday Shift

Level change during RTH from new volume, rolling, theta, or breakouts. Common in high-volume 0DTE environments (SPXW, SPY).

Dealer Hedging

Market makers delta-hedge customer option flow. Their buy/sell activity at key strikes creates the walls and flip levels shown on this page.

0DTE Gamma

Same-day expiration options carry extreme gamma near ATM. Walls can relocate quickly; always confirm with live flow before sizing SPXW trades.
