☑ Daily SPX Options Checklist (5–10 min)

  1. Overnight ES/NQ — gap vs prior RTH close; note size (<0.3% vs >0.8%).
  2. VIX level & change — regime for premium (sell vs buy vol). Open VIX Dashboard for detail.
  3. Rates / DXY — rising yields or strong dollar often pressure SPX, especially QQQ-heavy days.
  4. Mag 7 vs SPY — index can lag if heavyweights diverge.
  5. GEX / flows — GEX Profile for walls & flip; Heat Maps for regime zones.
  6. Economic calendar — CPI, NFP, FOMC: reduce size or widen strikes on high-impact days.
  7. Align ML + structure — trade when futures bias, vol regime, and GEX agree; fade when they conflict.

📖 How to Use This Data for SPX Options

Macros (VIX, yields, gold, oil)

  • VIX rising while ES flat → dealers hedging; favor defined-risk, smaller size on 0DTE.
  • Yields up + TLT down → growth/multiple pressure; watch QQQ vs SPY divergence.
  • Gold up + equities down → classic risk-off; put spreads or wait for stabilization.
  • Oil spike → inflation fear; can lift energy, hurt broad beta — check XLE vs SPY.

Futures (ES, NQ, RTY)

  • ES leads SPX — overnight direction sets opening bias for SPXW.
  • NQ > ES → growth-led rally; RTY > ES → broad risk-on / small-cap bid.
  • Large gap → expect mean-reversion or trend continuation after 10:00 ET; check GEX flip.

ETFs (SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA)

  • QQQ outperforming SPY → narrow leadership; SPX can look strong while breadth is weak.
  • IWM leading → healthier rally for short premium; fades have less follow-through.
  • Use ETF ML signals as confirmation, not sole entry trigger.

Mega-cap stocks

  • Top names are ~25%+ of SPX — if NVDA/AAPL red while SPY green, caution on calls.
  • Stock ML aligned with ES → higher conviction for directional SPXW.
  • Earnings on a mega-cap → trade reduced size or avoid 0DTE that day.

Currency (DXY, EUR/USD, USD/JPY)

  • Strong dollar (DXY up) → often tightens financial conditions; headwind for SPX.
  • EUR/USD up → typically risk-on USD liquidity; mild SPX tailwind.
  • USD/JPY down (yen strength) → can signal risk-off from carry unwind.

Putting it together

Score the session: +1 bullish for ES up, VIX down, QQQ≥SPY, DXY down; -1 bearish for the opposite. Net +2 or more → favor call structures above gamma flip; net -2 → favor puts or iron condors wide of spot. Always cross-check GEX levels before entry.

📚 Terminology

SPX / SPXW

SPX is the S&P 500 index (cash-settled options, AM settlement on some series). SPXW is the weekly PM-settled contract — popular for 0DTE. Both track the same index; liquidity and margin differ.

ES Futures

E-mini S&P 500 (ES) trades nearly 24 hours and is the primary vehicle for overnight price discovery. SPX options traders use ES for fair value and gap context before the cash open.

VIX

CBOE Volatility Index — 30-day implied vol of SPX options. It moves inversely to SPX most days. Rising VIX = higher option premiums and often negative gamma environment.

GEX (Gamma Exposure)

Estimated dealer net gamma by strike. Positive gamma above the flip level tends to dampen moves; negative gamma below amplifies trends. See GEX Profile for Call Wall, Put Wall, and Flip.

DXY — US Dollar Index

Measures USD vs a basket of currencies. Rising DXY often coincides with tighter global liquidity and pressure on US equities, especially multinationals and EM-sensitive risk.

2s/10s Spread

Difference between 10-year and 2-year Treasury yields. Inversion (negative spread) is a recession warning; steepening from inverted can coincide with volatility in equities.

0DTE / 1DTE

Options expiring same day or next session. Theta decay is extreme; gamma and vega matter more than for swing trades. Requires tight risk and clear regime read.

ML Signal (BUY / SELL / HOLD)

Gradient-boosting model on 4H bars: direction probability and magnitude. Use as confluence with macro bias and GEX — not as a standalone entry.

Risk-on / Risk-off

Risk-on: ES up, VIX down, credit tight, cyclicals lead. Risk-off: ES down, VIX up, bonds/gold bid, USD often firm. SPXW premium strategies behave very differently in each.

Breadth vs Cap-weight

SPX is cap-weighted. Strong index with weak IWM/small caps = narrow rally. Compare QQQ vs SPY and IWM vs SPY before assuming broad participation.

Overnight Gap

ES last price vs prior RTH close. Gaps >0.5% increase odds of fill or extension in the first hour — adjust strike selection and size accordingly.

Implied vs Realized Vol

If VIX (implied) is high vs recent SPX realized range, premium selling has edge if regime is positive gamma; if VIX is low into event risk, long vol may be cheaper.
