How to Read the GEX Profile
Market maker hedging mechanics & trading implications

🟢 Positive Gamma Regime

Spot is above the Gamma Flip. Dealers are net long gamma.

  • Market makers sell into rallies and buy dips
  • Price pins near Call/Put Walls; volatility suppressed
  • Bias: Buy dips to Put Wall, fade rallies to Call Wall

🔴 Negative Gamma Regime

Spot is below the Gamma Flip. Dealers are net short gamma.

  • Market makers buy rallies and sell dips — amplifies moves
  • Trending moves accelerate; VIX tends to spike
  • Bias: Follow momentum, trade breakouts

⚡ Gamma Flip (HVL)

The High Volatility Level — the regime pivot.

  • Daily close above HVL → compression, stable grind
  • Daily close below HVL → expansion, trending moves
  • Key signal: Back-to-back closes confirm regime

🎯 Reading the Profile Bars

The histogram on the chart right side shows gamma at each price.

  • Green bars = positive gamma (stabilizing zone)
  • Red bars = negative gamma (volatility amplifying zone)
  • Longer bars = more dealer hedging concentration at that level
  • Bars auto-track the visible price range when you scroll/zoom

🏢 Institutional Footprint

Large option volume at a strike signals institutional positioning.

  • Heavy call buying → dealers buy underlying (supportive)
  • Heavy put buying → dealers sell underlying (bearish)
  • Call Wall (green dashed line) = max call concentration
  • Put Wall (red dashed line) = max put concentration

🔥 Gamma Squeeze

Spot is above the Call Wall. Dealers must chase.

  • Dealers short gamma above Call Wall — forced to buy rallies
  • Self-reinforcing momentum, fast moves, shallow pullbacks
  • Resolves with sharp reversal when momentum exhausts
  • Risk: Never short a gamma squeeze early
