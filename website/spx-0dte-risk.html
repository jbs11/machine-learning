    Session Playbook
    Terminology & Framework

    VIX1D (1-Day Volatility Index)

    The market's expectation for SPX volatility over the next 24 hours — the cleanest read on 0DTE risk. <12 low risk (tight ranges, scalp exits). 12–18 normal. >20 fat-tail environment — cut size and widen spreads.

    Expected Move (EM)

    Derived from ATM 0DTE straddle mid-prices (scaled SPY → SPX). Marks the ~1σ boundary options price for the session. Breaking full EM with momentum invalidates range/mean-reversion theses (e.g. iron condors). Partial ±0.55% marks an early warning band.

    Gamma Exposure (GEX)

    Aggregate dealer gamma positioning. Positive GEX — dealers buy dips / sell rips, suppressing vol. Zero / negative GEX — hedging accelerates trends; fat-tail blowups more likely.

    Kurtosis & Z-Scores

    Excess kurtosis > 3 flags historically fat return distributions. |Z| > 3.5 signals an intraday move beyond normal ±2σ boundaries — treat as tail event, not mean-reversion by default.
