💡 Key Correlation Relationships for SPX Options Trading

VIX — Inverse Signal

VIX moves inversely to SPX (~-0.75 to -0.85 correlation). Rising VIX signals fear and potential SPX decline. VIX spikes above 20 often mark short-term SPX lows. Use for 0DTE directional bias.

ES Futures — Leading Indicator

ES futures trade nearly 24/7 and lead SPX price discovery. Pre-market ES direction is a reliable 0DTE/1DTE opening bias. ES/SPX correlation is near +1.0 during RTH. Monitor overnight gaps.

Treasury Yields / TLT

TLT (20Y bonds) correlates negatively with SPX in risk-off regimes. A flight to bonds (TLT rising) signals risk aversion. Rising rates (TLT falling) can pressure SPX, especially QQQ/growth stocks.

Mega-Cap Stocks

AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN comprise 20%+ of SPX weight. When mega-caps diverge from SPX, the index often follows. A lagging SPX with strong mega-caps = bullish; strong SPX with weak mega-caps = caution.

EUR/USD — Liquidity Proxy

EUR/USD reflects global USD liquidity. Weaker USD (EUR/USD rising) = risk-on, often bullish for SPX. Strong USD (EUR/USD falling) can signal risk-off. Useful for 1DTE–5DTE macro bias.
