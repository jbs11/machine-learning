Drop MP4 lesson videos here.

Naming convention:  course-MODULE-LESSON.mp4
Examples:
  course-1-1.mp4   (Module 1, Lesson 1)
  course-2-3.mp4   (Module 2, Lesson 3)

Then update course-data.js for that lesson:
  video: "/video/course-1-1.mp4"

For YouTube videos, set:
  video: "https://youtu.be/YOUR_VIDEO_ID"
