Technology & Trading Guide

OptionCharts-style analytics: chain, GEX, volume/OI, IV surface, and ML strategy hints. Data via yfinance (delayed unless IBKR connected). SPX uses SPY proxy at index price levels.

Tabs — definitions & how to trade

Option Chain — Full call/put grid. Trade liquid strikes (tight spread, high OI).

GEX — Dealer gamma by strike; walls and flip guide mean-reversion vs trend.

Volume / OI — Where flow and positioning concentrate; confirms support/resistance.

IV Skew / Term — Rich/cheap vol; pick spreads and expiry.

Max Pain / Prob — Expiry magnets and implied range for sizing condors/spreads.

P&L / Strategy — Compare structures; use Profit & Loss section to template multi-leg trades.

Glossary

ATM IV — Implied move expectation (annualized).
IV Rank — IV vs 1y range; high favors premium selling.
GEX regime — Positive: dampened moves; negative: amplified.
Gamma flip — Strike where net GEX crosses zero.
PCR — Put/call ratio; positioning bias indicator.
Expected move — ≈ spot × IV × √(DTE/365).
