📚 Terminology

10-Year Yield (^TNX / US10Y)

The benchmark long-rate. It prices growth, inflation expectations, and term premium. When the 10Y backs up sharply, growth multiples compress — SPX and QQQ often sell off; put skew and VIX can rise together.

2-Year Yield (US2Y)

The front-end “Fed path” rate. It reprices fastest on CPI, NFP, and FOMC. Large 2Y moves (especially 8–10+ bps/day) frequently arrive before vol expansion in SPX and hurt short-gamma structures.

2s/10s Spread

10Y minus 2Y. Curve shape matters as much as level:

  • Bear steepener — long end rises more → often risk-off for equities.
  • Bull steepener — short end falls on cut hopes → often risk-on if growth holds.
  • Flatteners — front-end-led moves; hawkish flatteners stress growth/tech.

Basis Points (bps)

0.01% = 1 bp. A 4.20% → 4.28% move is +8 bps. For options traders, track daily bps change in 2Y and 10Y, not just the level.

Yield Shock & Short Gamma

Sudden 8–10+ bps moves in 2Y or 10Y tend to coincide with directional SPX breaks and rising IV. Short straddles, iron condors, and tight butterflies face gap & vol risk — especially on event days.

Treasury Auctions (10Y / 30Y @ 1pm ET)

Supply hits the long end. Weak auctions can steepen the curve and pressure SPX into the close; strong auctions can relieve rates and support risk assets for the session.

🎯 Trading Playbook — Using Yields for SPX Options

Pre-Market & Event Checklist

  1. Check 2Y first. Overnight 2Y move sets the Fed narrative. If |Δ2Y| > 8 bps, plan for wider SPX ranges and higher IV — reduce short gamma size.
  2. Read the curve. Bear steepener → favor defined-risk puts / bear call spreads or sit out short vol. Bull steepener with strong ES → cautious call spreads / reduced put sizing.
  3. Align with GEX. Use GEX Profile — negative gamma + rising yields is a high-vol combo; positive gamma + stable yields favors range trades.
  4. Event days (CPI, NFP, FOMC). Avoid opening short premium into the print unless you have explicit vol edge. Consider 0DTE only after the reaction if structure is clear.
  5. Auction afternoons. On 10Y/30Y auction days, watch 1:00–3:00 PM ET for curve steepening and SPX drift — gamma on 0DTE can accelerate into the close.
  6. VIX confirmation. If yields spike and VIX rises 5%+ while SPX breaks a key level, treat it as a vol expansion regime — not a fade-the-move environment.
